Category rules

"When a transaction looks like X, it is Y." Rules run on every new transaction. Lower priority numbers run first; the first match wins. Tick "Always categorize like this" in the inbox to create one in a click.

What runs automatically on every import (and on ⚡ Auto-categorize), in this order:

  1. Your rules, then the starter rules (priority 200). Rules marked suggest only pre-fill the category for you to confirm.
  2. Transfers: the same amount going out of one of your accounts and into another within 3 days is paired as a transfer — card payments, moves to savings.
  3. Matches: an outflow equal to exactly one unpaid supplier bill, a deposit equal to exactly one customer payment, or an amount equal to one hand-entered expense is linked to it.
  4. Vendors you've saved pre-fill their default category.
  5. What you did before: a merchant you've categorized the same way 3+ times is categorized for real; fewer times, it's suggested.

Everything auto-applied keeps an "auto" mark and can be undone from the inbox.
