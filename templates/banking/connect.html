Good to know

Opening balances for connected accounts are set from the bank on the first sync, so the balance here matches your bank from day one.

Pending charges show with a pending tag and are replaced when they post. They do not count toward balances until then.

If a bank asks you to log in again, the connection shows Re-login needed; press Re-login to fix it.
