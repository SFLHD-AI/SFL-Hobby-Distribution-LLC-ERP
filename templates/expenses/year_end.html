Cash vs accrual, in plain words

Cash basis (what your tax return uses): an expense counts in the year the money left — the paid date, or the date of the bank/card transaction it came from.

Accrual basis (what the business really cost): an expense counts in the year of its bill date, paid or not. Bills still open at year end are the difference between the two.

The package has both columns side by side, and the README inside explains each file.
