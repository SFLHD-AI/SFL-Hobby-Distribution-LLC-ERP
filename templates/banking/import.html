How it works

1. In your bank's website, download transactions as CSV for the date range you need.

2. Upload it here. The bank format is detected from the column names (Chase, Amex, Capital One, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citi, Discover, PayPal, Square). Any other bank works too — you pick the columns.

3. Check the preview, then import. Rows you already have are skipped, so re-uploading an overlapping statement is safe.

4. New rows run through your rules; the rest wait in the inbox.
